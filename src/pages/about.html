About {{site.name}}

{{site.name}} is a collection of free online tools for everyday tasks: compressing and converting images, merging and splitting PDFs, counting words, generating QR codes and passwords, formatting JSON and more.

Our principles

  • Privacy first. Every tool runs entirely in your web browser. Your files and text are processed on your own device and are never uploaded to our servers.
  • Free, with no catches. No accounts, no watermarks, no daily limits. The site is supported by non-intrusive advertising.
  • Fast and simple. Each tool does one job well, loads quickly and works on phones, tablets and computers.

Why we built it

Too many "free" online tools upload your private documents to unknown servers, add watermarks, or push you into a subscription after one use. We wanted tools that are genuinely free and that respect your privacy, built with modern browser technology so nothing needs to leave your device.
