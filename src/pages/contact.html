Reporting a problem

To help us fix issues quickly, please include:

  • The name of the tool you were using
  • Your browser and device (for example "Chrome on Windows" or "Safari on iPhone")
  • What you expected to happen and what happened instead

Please don't send us private files. Because our tools run in your browser, we never need your documents to troubleshoot.
