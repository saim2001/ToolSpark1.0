All of our tools run entirely in your web browser. Files you select (images, PDFs and others) and text you enter are processed locally on your device. They are not uploaded to, stored on, or viewable by our servers. When you close or refresh the page, that data is gone.

Some tools load open-source code libraries (for example, for PDF processing or QR code generation) from public content delivery networks such as cdnjs. These providers may receive your IP address when the library loads, but they do not receive your files or text.
