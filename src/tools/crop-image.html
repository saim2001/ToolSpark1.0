How to crop an image

  1. Choose or drop the photo you want to crop.
  2. Drag the corners of the box to resize it, or drag inside the box to move it. You can also drag anywhere on the photo to draw a new selection.
  3. Pick an aspect ratio to lock the shape, for example 1:1 for profile pictures or 16:9 for YouTube thumbnails.
  4. Choose a format and click Crop & download. The crop uses the full original resolution.

Frequently asked questions

Does cropping reduce quality?

No. The selected area is cut out at the original pixel resolution, with no resampling. Choose PNG for a lossless result.

The grid lines in the crop box — what are they for?

They divide the selection into thirds. Placing your subject on one of the lines or intersections (the "rule of thirds") usually gives a more balanced photo.

Is my photo uploaded?

No. Cropping happens in your browser, so your image stays on your device.

Can I crop to an exact pixel size?

Crop to the right aspect ratio here, then use our Image Resizer to set the exact width and height.
