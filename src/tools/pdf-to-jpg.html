How to convert a PDF to JPG

    1. Choose the PDF file you want to convert.
    2. Pick JPG (smaller files) or PNG (sharpest text and lines).
    3. Choose a resolution: 150 DPI is great for screens and sharing, while 300 DPI suits printing.
    4. Optionally list only the pages you need, then click Convert to images.
    5. Download single pages or all images at once.

    Why convert PDF pages to images?

    • Share on social media or chat apps, which show images inline but not PDFs.
    • Insert a page into slides or documents as a picture.
    • Upload to sites that only accept JPG/PNG, such as some application portals and marketplaces.
    • Create thumbnails or previews of reports, menus and brochures.

    Frequently asked questions

    JPG or PNG — which is better?

    JPG files are much smaller and ideal for pages with photos. PNG is lossless and keeps small text and thin lines crisp, but files are larger.

    What DPI should I choose?

    72 DPI matches the PDF's on-screen size. 150 DPI doubles the detail and suits most uses. 300 DPI is print quality, but creates large files and takes longer for long documents.

    Is my PDF uploaded?

    No. Pages are rendered by your browser using the open-source pdf.js engine, so your document stays on your device.
