How to rotate or flip an image

  1. Choose the image you want to fix.
  2. Click Rotate left or Rotate right to turn it 90° at a time, or use the flip buttons to mirror it.
  3. To straighten a tilted horizon, drag the fine angle slider. The extra corners are filled with the background you choose.
  4. Click Download to save the result at full resolution.

Why is my photo sideways?

Phones often save photos in the orientation the sensor captured, plus a small "rotate me" tag (EXIF orientation). Some apps, websites and older software ignore that tag, so the photo appears sideways. Rotating it here bakes the correct orientation into the pixels, so it displays correctly everywhere.

Frequently asked questions

Does rotating by 90° lose quality?

Rotating by 90° or 180° moves pixels without blending them, so no detail is lost. Saving as JPG re-compresses the image, so choose PNG if you need a perfect copy.

When should I flip an image?

Front cameras often save mirrored selfies, so text on your shirt reads backwards. Use Flip horizontal to fix it. Flipping is also handy for design layouts that need a subject facing the other way.

Is my image uploaded?

No. Everything happens in your browser, and your image never leaves your device.
