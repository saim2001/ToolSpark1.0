How to merge PDF files

    1. Select or drop two or more PDF files into the box above.
    2. Arrange them in the order you want using the ↑ and ↓ buttons.
    3. Click Merge PDFs. Your combined file downloads immediately.

    Why merge PDFs in your browser?

    Most online PDF tools upload your documents to their servers. Contracts, bank statements, invoices and ID scans are sensitive, so {{site.name}} merges files locally on your device. That's faster, too: there's no upload or download wait, even for large files.

    Common uses

    • Combine invoices or receipts for an expense report.
    • Join chapters or scanned pages into one document.
    • Bundle a CV, cover letter and certificates into a single application file.
    • Merge signed contract pages back into the full agreement.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is there a file size limit?

    No limit is imposed by us. Merging hundreds of megabytes works on most computers; phones may struggle with extremely large files.

    Will the quality change?

    No. Pages are copied exactly as they are, so text, images and formatting are untouched.

    Can I merge password-protected PDFs?

    Encrypted PDFs need to be unlocked first. Open them with the password and "print to PDF" or save an unprotected copy, then merge.

    Can I remove pages before merging?

    Yes. Use our Split PDF tool to extract only the pages you need, then merge the results here.
