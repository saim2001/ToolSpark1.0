How to rotate PDF pages

  1. Choose your PDF. A preview of every page appears.
  2. Click any page to turn it 90° clockwise. Click again to keep turning it.
  3. Or use Rotate all left/right to turn every page at once.
  4. Click Save rotated PDF. The rotation is saved permanently, so it looks right in every PDF viewer.

Common reasons to rotate a PDF

  • Scanned pages that came out sideways or upside down.
  • A landscape table or chart mixed into a portrait document.
  • Phone scans where a few pages were captured in the wrong orientation.

Frequently asked questions

Does rotating affect quality?

No. Rotation only changes the page's orientation setting. Text, images and links are untouched, and text stays selectable.

Is my PDF uploaded?

No. Previews and rotation are handled in your browser, and your file never leaves your device.

Can I delete or reorder pages too?

Yes. Use Split PDF to extract or reorder pages, and Merge PDF to combine files.
