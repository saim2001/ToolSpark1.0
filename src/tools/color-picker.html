How to use the color picker

  • Choose a color with the picker, or type any HEX, RGB or HSL value. The other formats update instantly.
  • Pick from an image: upload a photo, logo or screenshot and click any pixel to get its exact color.
  • Pick from screen (Chrome and Edge): grab a color from anywhere on your screen.
  • Tints & shades give you lighter and darker versions of your color, which is handy for building a palette or hover states.

Color contrast and accessibility

The contrast check shows how readable white or black text would be on your color, using the WCAG 2 contrast ratio. A ratio of at least 4.5:1 passes AA for normal text, and 3:1 passes for large text (24px+, or 19px+ bold). Good contrast makes your design readable for everyone, including people with low vision.

Frequently asked questions

Is my image uploaded when I pick colors from it?

No. The image is loaded into your browser only, and nothing is sent anywhere.

Why isn't "Pick from screen" showing?

It uses the EyeDropper API, which is currently available in Chrome, Edge and Opera on desktop. On other browsers, take a screenshot and use "Pick a color from an image" instead.
