How to compress a PDF

  1. Choose the PDF that's too large.
  2. Pick a compression level. Recommended keeps text readable while cutting the size of most scans by 50–90%.
  3. Click Compress PDF. You'll see the old and new size before downloading.

How it works (and when to use it)

This tool re-draws each page as an optimized image at a lower resolution and re-builds the PDF. That makes it very effective for:

  • Scanned documents and photos of paperwork from your phone
  • Image-heavy PDFs such as brochures, portfolios and photo reports
  • Getting under upload limits on email (often 20–25 MB), job portals and government sites (often 1–2 MB)

Good to know: because pages become images, text in the compressed PDF can't be selected or searched, and links stop working. For text-only PDFs exported from Word or Google Docs, the original is usually already small. If compression wouldn't help, we'll tell you.

Frequently asked questions

Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

No. Unlike most PDF compressors, everything happens in your browser. That matters for bank statements, contracts and ID documents.

Which level should I choose?

Start with Recommended. If the file is still too big, try Extreme or turn on grayscale. If small text looks blurry, use Light.

Why did my file get bigger?

PDFs made of pure text and vector graphics are already compact, so turning them into images can increase the size. In that case, keep your original file.
