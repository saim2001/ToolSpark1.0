Frequently asked questions

What's the difference between GB and GiB?

A gigabyte (GB) is 1,000,000,000 bytes, the decimal unit used by drive makers. A gibibyte (GiB) is 1,073,741,824 bytes (2³⁰), which Windows reports as "GB". That's why a 1 TB drive shows about 931 GB on a PC.

Are US and UK gallons the same?

No. A US gallon is 3.785 liters, and an imperial (UK) gallon is 4.546 liters, about 20% larger. Both are available in the volume converter.

How accurate are the conversions?

Conversions use the exact international definitions (for example, 1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly), and results are shown to 10 significant digits.
