How to convert images

    1. Add one or more images by dropping them in the box or clicking to browse.
    2. Choose the format you want: JPG, PNG or WebP.
    3. For JPG and WebP, set the quality (90% looks identical to the original for most images).
    4. Download each converted file or use Download all.

    Popular conversions

    • WebP to JPG — open images saved from websites in any app or photo editor.
    • PNG to JPG — make screenshots and graphics much smaller for email and uploads.
    • JPG to WebP — speed up your website with files 25–35% smaller.
    • JPG to PNG — get a lossless copy for editing without further quality loss.
    • SVG to PNG — turn a vector logo into an image you can paste anywhere.

    Frequently asked questions

    Can I convert HEIC photos from my iPhone?

    HEIC support depends on your browser. Safari can open HEIC files, so on a Mac or iPhone you can convert them to JPG here. Chrome and Firefox on Windows usually cannot read HEIC yet.

    What happens to transparent backgrounds?

    PNG and WebP keep transparency. JPG doesn't support it, so transparent areas are filled with white.

    Are animated GIFs supported?

    Only the first frame of an animated GIF is converted, since JPG and PNG are still-image formats.

    Is it safe for private images?

    Yes. Conversion runs locally in your browser and nothing is uploaded to any server.
