How to resize an image

  1. Choose or drop the image you want to resize.
  2. Type a new width or height. With Keep aspect ratio on, the other side updates automatically so the image is never stretched.
  3. Or click a preset: 25%, 50%, 75%, common web widths, or a 1080×1080 square for Instagram.
  4. Pick an output format and click Resize & download.

Frequently asked questions

Will resizing reduce image quality?

Making an image smaller keeps it sharp. Making it larger than the original cannot add detail, so enlarged images may look soft. For best results, only scale down.

Does the aspect ratio lock crop my image?

No. With the lock on, the image is scaled proportionally. If you turn it off and enter both sides, the image will be stretched to fit exactly — nothing is cropped.

Is my image uploaded?

No. Resizing is done by your browser on your own device. The image is never sent anywhere.

What happens to transparency?

PNG and WebP keep transparent areas. JPG does not support transparency, so transparent areas become white.
