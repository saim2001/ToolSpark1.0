How to remove duplicate lines

  1. Paste your text into the Input box, with one item per line.
  2. Duplicates are removed instantly and the unique lines appear in Result.
  3. Adjust the options if needed, then copy or download the cleaned list.

Options

  • Ignore case treats "Apple" and "apple" as the same line. The first version found is kept.
  • Trim spaces ignores spaces at the start and end of lines, so "apple " matches "apple".
  • Remove empty lines deletes blank lines from the result.
  • Sort orders the result alphabetically, with natural number ordering (2 comes before 10).

What people use it for

  • Cleaning email lists before importing them into a newsletter tool.
  • De-duplicating SEO keyword lists, URLs or hashtags.
  • Removing repeated entries from exported spreadsheet columns or log files.

Frequently asked questions

How many lines can it handle?

Hundreds of thousands of lines are processed in a fraction of a second on most devices.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything runs locally in your browser and nothing you paste is uploaded.
