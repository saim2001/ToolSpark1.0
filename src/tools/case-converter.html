Frequently asked questions

How does Title Case handle small words?

Short words like "a", "and", "of", "the" and "in" stay lowercase unless they are the first or last word, following common headline style guides (AP and Chicago).

I typed with caps lock on by accident. How do I fix it?

Click iNVERSE cASE to flip every letter, or Sentence case to rewrite it normally.

Is my text stored?

No. Conversion happens instantly in your browser and nothing is sent to a server.
